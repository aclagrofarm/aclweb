Growing Agriculture. Embracing Innovation. Building Tomorrow.

ACL Agro Farm Ltd. is an agriculture-focused company based in Nepal, engaged in mixed agriculture and modern farming. We are committed to developing our agricultural activities, exploring practical farming solutions, and creating opportunities for sustainable growth in the agricultural sector.

We believe agriculture is more than food production. It supports livelihoods, strengthens communities, creates business opportunities, and plays an important role in the future of our economy. We aim to combine practical agricultural knowledge with modern farming approaches to build a business that grows responsibly and adapts to changing agricultural needs.

Our Story

Rooted in Agriculture. Focused on the Future.

Agriculture has the potential to create lasting value when practical experience, responsible management, and new ideas work together.

At ACL Agro Farm Ltd., our journey is centered on this belief. Operating in Nepal's agricultural sector, we are developing our business around mixed agriculture and modern farming, with an emphasis on continuous improvement and long-term development.

We recognize that farming is an evolving field. Changing market needs, resource management, production efficiency, and access to appropriate technology all influence the way agricultural businesses grow. We therefore believe in remaining adaptable, learning from experience, and exploring farming approaches suited to our operations and local conditions.

At ACL Agro Farm Ltd., we see every stage of growth as an opportunity to learn, improve, and contribute to the future of agriculture.
