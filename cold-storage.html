Cold Storage

Post-harvest preservation and market-linked supply systems for safer agricultural distribution.

Post-Harvest Value

Cold storage supports product preservation, reduces food waste, and helps link farm production to stronger market opportunities. This allows growers to manage seasonal supply more effectively.

  • Food preservation and reduced spoilage
  • Market-linked agricultural logistics
  • Post-harvest handling systems
  • Improved product quality and shelf life
