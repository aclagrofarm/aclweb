01 / OUR APPROACH

Practical planning for a stronger farm economy.

ACL Agro Farm Ltd. is focused on mixed agriculture and modern farming in Nepal. As the company continues to develop and expand its agricultural activities, we are interested in exploring suitable business partnerships and opportunities that support responsible agricultural growth.

Our approach is centered on practical planning, transparent communication, effective farm management, and the long-term development of agricultural enterprises.

We welcome enquiries from individuals, businesses, agricultural entrepreneurs, and potential partners interested in learning more about our proposed projects and collaboration opportunities.

04 / COLLABORATION

Partnership Models

Depending on the project and applicable legal requirements, potential collaboration may be explored through the following models:

Project-Based Collaboration

Cooperation on a defined agricultural project with agreed objectives, responsibilities, budget, and reporting arrangements.

Operational Partnership

Collaboration in farm management, technical operations, production planning, or other agreed business activities.

Equipment or Infrastructure Partnership

Cooperation involving suitable agricultural equipment, infrastructure, or other resources under a documented agreement.

Equity or Capital Participation

Any shareholding or capital-participation arrangement would be considered only after reviewing the company's legal structure, approvals, valuation, ownership terms, and applicable laws.

No partnership or investment arrangement is confirmed until the relevant parties have completed due diligence and signed the required documents.

06 / TRANSPARENCY

Transparency and Risk Disclosure

Agriculture and livestock businesses are exposed to production, disease, weather, market-price, operating-cost, financing, and other risks.

Financial estimates, where available, should be accompanied by their assumptions, cost breakdowns, production estimates, and limitations. Past performance or projected figures do not guarantee future results.

ACL Agro Farm Ltd. does not represent any unverified financial projection as a guaranteed return. Potential partners should independently review the available information and obtain appropriate financial and legal advice before making a commitment.

07 / QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an investment opportunity currently open?

Availability must be confirmed by ACL Agro Farm Ltd. after reviewing the relevant project, its readiness, and applicable requirements.

What is the minimum investment amount?

The minimum amount, if applicable, depends on the specific project and the legally approved arrangement. Please request the relevant project information.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Agricultural business outcomes depend on operating performance, market conditions, costs, and other risks. No return should be assumed unless a legally valid agreement expressly provides for a specific obligation.
